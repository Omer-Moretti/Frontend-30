Ingredients

  • 60g of wheat flour
  • 1 egg
  • 10 gram of baking powder
  • 100 gram of banana

Instructions

  1. Take all the ingredients and mash them together very good until its get smoother/creamy texture
  2. Fire up your pan on a low heat
  3. pour the mixture and make sure it is in rounded shape
  4. Wait until you see bubles forming in the surface
  5. Flip the pancake and cook until golden brown
  6. Take it from pan and wait for for it cool down
  7. Enjoy your pancake with the sous or syrup as you wish

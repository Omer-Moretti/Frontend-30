Day 1

Log time:7 august
Notes of the day
Lamp lit 16:20. Wind SW, moderate. Visibility 4 miles. No vessels sighted. Nothing on the water.

Day 2

Log time:8 august
Notes of the day
Lamp lit 16:18. Wind calm, sea flat. Two fishing boats passed at dusk, made no signal. Quiet night.

Day 3

Log time:9 august
Notes of the day
Lamp lit 16:15. Fog from the north after midnight. Sounded the horn twice an hour until first light.

Day 4

Log time:10 august
Notes of the day
Lamp lit 16:11. Relief boat did not come. Stores adequate. Wind rising from the west.

Day 5

Log time:11 august
Notes of the day

Lamp lit 16:08. Relief boat did not come. Something struck the rock below the east gallery around 02:00. Found nothing at dawn. Marked the stone where the paint is scraped.

Day 6

Log time:12 august
Notes of the day
Lamp lit 16:04. Gale, force 9. The horn sounded three times in the night. I did not sound it.

Day 7

Log time: 13 august
Notes of the day
Lamp lit 16:01. Sea calm as glass, no wind at all after the gale, which is not usual. The light held steady until 03:40 and then dimmed, though the oil was full and the wick was trimmed at midnight. I went up. The lens was turned inward, facing the stair. It cannot turn that way.
